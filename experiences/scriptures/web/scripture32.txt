“And it came to pass that the voice of the Lord
came to them in their afflictions, saying:
[Lift up your heads](glow) and be of good comfort,
for I know of the [covenant](underline) which ye have made unto me;
and I will covenant with my people
and [deliver them out of bondage](highlight).

And I will also [ease the burdens](script)
which are put upon your shoulders,
that even you cannot feel them upon your backs,
even while you are in bondage;
and this will I do that ye may [stand as witnesses](underline) for me,
and that ye may know of a surety
that I, the Lord God, do [visit my people](circle)
in their afflictions.”
